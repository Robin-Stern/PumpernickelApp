## Decisions Made

- **Layout: bullets, no sub-tables.** A small per-ingredient table was permitted by the plan ("ONLY if it makes the per-ingredient math clearer than bullets") but bullets stayed under the line budget cleanly and aligned visually with the surrounding sections, which all use bullets. Final body is 25 lines of markdown (top of the allowed band).
- **Bold emphasis on phase labels (`**First draft:**`, `**Sum**`, `**Check**`, `**Adjustment**`, `**Re-sum**`, `**Re-check**`).** Gives the reader (and the model) clear phase boundaries without adding sub-headings that would inflate the heading count or compete with the existing `##` structure.
- **Used `(per-100g × amountGrams / 100)` once as a formula reminder, then dropped the `/ 100` from the per-line arithmetic.** Showing the formula once anchors the math; repeating `/ 100` four more times would have padded word count without adding pedagogical value.
- **Closing sentence verbatim from the plan**, no paraphrase or softening — the plan specified `verbatim` for this line.
